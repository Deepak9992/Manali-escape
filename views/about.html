Our Story

About Manali Escape

Who We Are

Manali Escape is a local tourism company based in the heart of Himachal Pradesh. We are a team of passionate Himalayan locals who have grown up among these mountains and know every trail, temple, and hidden waterfall by heart.

We started Manali Escape with one simple goal — to help travellers experience the real Himachal Pradesh. Not just the tourist spots, but the quiet villages, the ancient monasteries, the star-lit skies of Spiti, and the warmth of local hospitality.

What We Do

We offer curated travel experiences across Himachal Pradesh — from adventure treks and paragliding to heritage tours and wellness retreats. We also help travellers find the best local hotels, trusted taxi drivers, and honest travel advice through our blog.
