Introduction

Manali is one of India's most popular hill stations, located in the beautiful Kullu Valley of Himachal Pradesh. Surrounded by snow-capped mountains, pine forests, apple orchards, and the Beas River, it is an excellent destination for families, honeymooners, adventure lovers, and backpackers. Whether you are looking for snowfall, thrilling adventure sports, or a peaceful mountain escape, Manali offers unforgettable experiences throughout the year.

Quick Information

LocationManali, Himachal Pradesh
AltitudeApprox. 2,050 m
Nearest AirportBhuntar Airport (50 km)
Best TimeMarch–June & December–February

Best Time to Visit

Summer (March–June)

Perfect for sightseeing, trekking, river rafting, camping, and paragliding with pleasant temperatures.

Monsoon (July–September)

Beautiful greenery but occasional landslides and road closures may occur.

Winter (October–February)

The best season to enjoy snowfall, skiing, and winter photography.

How to Reach Manali

  • By Road: Volvo buses and self-drive routes from Delhi and Chandigarh.
  • By Air: Bhuntar Airport followed by taxi.
  • By Train: Chandigarh Railway Station is the preferred rail connection.

Top Places to Visit

Solang Valley

Popular for skiing, paragliding, ziplining, ATV rides, and snow activities.

Atal Tunnel

An engineering marvel connecting Manali with Lahaul Valley throughout the year.

Sissu

Known for waterfalls, mountain scenery, and peaceful landscapes.

Old Manali

Famous for cafés, live music, shopping, and backpacker culture.

Hadimba Devi Temple

A historic wooden temple surrounded by cedar forests.

Vashisht Village

Home to natural hot water springs and traditional Himachali culture.

Travel Tips

  • Carry warm clothes throughout the year.
  • Book accommodation in advance during peak season.
  • Start sightseeing early to avoid traffic.
  • Drive carefully on mountain roads.
  • Support local businesses and keep the environment clean.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many days are enough?

Four to five days are ideal for exploring Manali and nearby attractions.

When does it snow in Manali?

Snowfall is most common from late December to early February.

Is Manali suitable for families?

Yes. It offers attractions and activities suitable for all age groups.
