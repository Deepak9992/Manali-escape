Rohtang Pass Travel Guide 2026 – Everything You Need to Know Before Visiting If you're planning a trip to Manali, visiting Rohtang Pass should definitely be on your bucket list. Surrounded by snow-covered mountains, beautiful valleys, and breathtaking views, Rohtang Pass is one of the most popular tourist attractions in Himachal Pradesh. Located about 51 km from Manali, Rohtang Pass sits at an altitude of around 13,000 feet above sea level. The journey itself is an adventure, with winding mountain roads, waterfalls, and stunning Himalayan landscapes all along the way. Whether you're visiting with family, friends, or your partner, Rohtang Pass offers an unforgettable experience. Why is Rohtang Pass So Famous? Rohtang Pass is famous for its natural beauty and snow-covered landscapes. Many tourists visit Manali mainly to experience snow, and Rohtang Pass is one of the best places to do that. The pass connects the beautiful Kullu Valley with the Lahaul and Spiti regions. As you travel towards Rohtang, you'll witness changing landscapes, from green valleys to rocky mountains and snow-covered peaks. The fresh mountain air and stunning views make every moment worth it. Best Time to Visit Rohtang Pass in 2026 The best time to visit Rohtang Pass is between May and October. May to June This is the most popular season for tourists. Snow can still be seen in many areas, and the weather is pleasant for sightseeing and outdoor activities. July to September The mountains become greener after the monsoon rains. While the scenery looks beautiful, travelers should check weather conditions before visiting. October October offers clear skies, fewer crowds, and amazing views of the surrounding mountains. It is one of the best months for photography. How to Reach Rohtang Pass Rohtang Pass is around 51 km from Manali and can be reached by: • Taxi • Private vehicle • Bike rental • Tour package The journey usually takes around 2 to 3 hours depending on traffic and weather conditions. Many travelers prefer starting early in the morning to avoid crowds and enjoy the scenic drive. What Can You Do at Rohtang Pass? One of the best things about Rohtang Pass is that there is something for everyone. Enjoy Snow Activities Even during summer, tourists can often find snow in certain areas. Popular activities include: • Snow scooter rides • Skiing • Snow tubing • Playing in the snow • Photography For many visitors from warmer parts of India, this is their first experience with snow. Take Amazing Photos Rohtang Pass is a paradise for photographers. Every turn offers a new view of: • Snow-covered mountains • Deep valleys • Glaciers • Waterfalls • Himalayan roads Don't forget to carry a fully charged phone or camera because you'll want to capture every moment. Enjoy the Scenic Drive The road from Manali to Rohtang Pass is one of the most beautiful drives in Himachal Pradesh. Along the route, you'll pass: • Pine forests • Flowing rivers • Small mountain villages • Waterfalls • Snow-covered peaks The journey is just as memorable as the destination itself. Places to Visit Near Rohtang Pass Solang Valley Located on the way to Rohtang Pass, Solang Valley is famous for adventure sports like paragliding, skiing, ziplining, and ATV rides. Atal Tunnel One of India's engineering marvels, the Atal Tunnel has made travel to the Lahaul Valley much easier and faster. Sissu A peaceful village known for its waterfall, mountain scenery, and relaxed atmosphere. Lahaul Valley Perfect for travelers who want to explore traditional Himalayan culture and untouched natural beauty. Important Travel Tips Before visiting Rohtang Pass, keep these tips in mind: • Carry warm clothes even during summer. • Wear comfortable shoes. • Carry sunglasses and sunscreen. • Start your journey early in the morning. • Check weather conditions before traveling. • Keep drinking water and snacks with you. • Follow local rules and help keep the mountains clean.
